Questions

Do you handle our money or deal?

No. We introduce verified people. You decide everything together, and no money passes through us.

Why only my city?

You can meet, visit each other's work and ask around. That builds trust faster than any online check.

Can anyone see my phone number?

No. Numbers are shared only after the other person accepts your request. Each member can send 5 new requests a month.

Do you store my Aadhaar or PAN?

No. We only record that you are verified, not your ID details.
